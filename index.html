About the shop

Sergio Oliva, on the job

Sergio’s Landscape and Lawn Care is a full-service commercial and residential landscaping contractor serving the Metro Austin area, including Pflugerville, Round Rock, Georgetown, Cedar Park, Hutto, Manor, Westlake, and Leander.

With 10 years of experience in designing, installing, repairing, and maintaining landscapes, owner-operator Sergio Oliva offers personalized, professional service to each client. The business is locally owned and operated.

In addition to landscape design, installation, and repair, Sergio uses landscape lighting to enhance a current design. Preparing for summer is an important step in maintaining a lawn and garden here in Texas. He is skilled in summer procedures for landscapes that have to stand up to harsh Texas weather.

Reliable. Trustworthy. Professional. Experienced. And if something extra comes up, customers say he will tell you whether he can do it — or point you to someone who can.
